I. The Cartesian problem, before it broke

A functional chord progression sitting safely inside C Major is a purely Cartesian frame. You have your basis vectors: Dm7 (ii), G7 (V), Cmaj7 (I). Legible, overdetermined. The ear doesn't need to argue with the coordinate system because the tonic holds it in place.

II. The Jacobian mapping between frames

What she does at [00:44] breaks the Cartesian frame entirely. You can't just add more numbers to C Major. You have to switch jurisdictions entirely. By altering a single invariant—flattening the E to Eb to turn Cmaj7 into Cm7—the I is transformed into a ii.

This operation is the Jacobian: the matrix telling you how a change in one key's frame propagates into another. The ii-V-I is no longer a localized event; it is the mathematical bridge that compresses the complexity of 12TET into a repeatable transform. Cmaj7 doesn't resolve; it's a toll booth you pass through to get to Bb Major.

III. Hessian — the modal flip

If II is the smooth relation between keys, III is the shock of the transition. When you hit Cmaj7, the ear expects rest. The sudden injection of the minor 3rd (the Eb) is the Hessian event. It is a sudden acceleration of harmonic gravity.

It's not a coordinate you stand in; it's the gap detector firing. The system realizes the strain has accelerated past what the C Major frame can absorb, forcing the ear to re-orient its center of gravity. The resolution was a lie.

IV. Bayesian — the tonic is not a noun

The listener's ear acts as the Bayesian prior. The first time the progression hits Cmaj7, the ear settles. When Cmaj7 becomes Cm7, the prior is violated and updated. The ear learns: the tonic is just a dominant in disguise. The posterior keeps re-forming with each new ii-V-I, realizing that "home" is an illusion.

V. Nietzschean — eternal recurrence

Because the 12TET is a closed circle, if you run this Jacobian operation 12 times, you end up exactly where you started. There is no final resolution, no utopian chord where the progression finally stops. It wills the whole cycle again, undanced by any promise of resolution.

You don't play the 12TET to finish it. You play it to affirm that the cycle itself is the only architecture there is.
